Four CFO personas

How it runs

Automation does the repetitive work, people review every reply

n8n workflow
  1. Build the target list from public company and professional data, tagged by persona.
  2. Check each contact against a suppression list.
  3. Personalise approved templates with verified fields only.
  4. Schedule touches within daily send limits.
  5. Detect replies, classify them, and alert a human.
  6. Hand qualified leads to Elite EPM with the conversation history.
  7. Report weekly on sends, replies and meetings booked.
Guardrails we state up front
  • LinkedInIts user agreement restricts bots and scraping, so volume stays low, tooling is approved, and a human sends where required.
  • WhatsAppBusiness messaging needs opt-in and approved templates, so cold WhatsApp is used only after a prior reply or consent.
  • Data protectionOnly what B2B outreach needs, a clear opt-out on every message, deletion requests honoured, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Counsel confirms the final approach.
  • Brand safetyAll copy is approved by Elite EPM, and nothing claims a relationship or customer result that is not verified.
